Good to know

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations before you commit.

Is the website demo really free?

Yes. If you're considering a new website, I can create a no-obligation design demo so you can see the direction before paying. For add-ons to an existing website, we'll first discuss your current setup and what can be connected; a full website demo isn't part of that path.

Can I keep my current website and domain?

Yes. Existing-site add-ons are intended to work with your current website and hosting, subject to access and compatibility. If you choose a new website instead, you can generally use a domain you own; I'll review the connection with you.

What do the monthly fees cover?

New website plans include the ongoing website service and the capabilities listed in your tier; even Website Essentials includes CRM, booking, and standard (non-AI) chat. Existing-site plans cover the connected tools and service listed in that tier, not your current site's hosting. Neither Essentials plan has SMS, AI, or phone usage charges. In higher tiers, applicable SMS, AI, or phone usage is billed separately at cost and varies with activity.

How long does setup take?

A new website can typically launch in about a week after you approve the design, depending on content and domain readiness. Add-on timing depends on the tools selected and the access available on your existing site; we'll confirm a timeline together.

What access do you need from me?

For a new website, I'll need your business details, content, and access to connect your domain when it's time to launch. For add-ons, I'll review your site's embed options or administrator access and any relevant booking or phone requirements before recommending a setup.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, we can talk through a higher tier or additional tools as your needs change. Any change in scope and pricing is discussed before work begins.

What happens after I choose a plan?

Email or call me first. We'll confirm fit, scope, any required integrations, and the applicable fees. For new websites, you'll review the free demo before payment; once you approve and payment is arranged, I'll complete the agreed setup and launch. There is no live checkout or connected platform form on this page.
