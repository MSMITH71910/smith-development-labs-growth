01 / What these terms cover

These terms apply to browsing the informational Smith Development Labs website and using its contact, chat, and booking options. They are not terms for buying website development, hosting, software, or other paid services.

02 / Inquiries, chat, and bookings

You can use the site to ask about a project, send a message, chat, or request a meeting. Please give accurate contact information if you would like a reply, and avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

Submitting an inquiry, using a chat tool, or selecting a booking time starts a conversation only. It does not create a purchase, reserve a paid service, or automatically form a client agreement. We may contact you about the request you made. For information about how inquiry details may be handled, see the Privacy Policy.

03 / Plans, prices, and paid work

Descriptions of services, plans, prices, and estimated timing on this site are informational starting points, not a final offer or a promise that a particular scope or price applies to your project. Mike will discuss your needs and confirm the proposed scope and charges before any paid work.

Any terms about deliverables, project ownership, payment, refunds, cancellation, warranties, or timelines would need to be set out in a separate written agreement before paid services begin. These website terms do not decide those matters.

04 / Site content and accuracy

Site text, visuals, examples, and design demos are provided to help you evaluate the work and start a discussion. Design concepts shown here are examples, not representations of active client projects unless identified otherwise. Please ask if you need to confirm whether a particular feature, example, or price applies to your situation.

We aim to keep information useful and current, but details may change. If you notice something that seems incorrect, please let us know.

Please use the site and its communication tools for genuine inquiries and lawful purposes. Do not submit abusive, misleading, or harmful material, interfere with the site's operation, attempt unauthorized access, or use the content in a way that misrepresents Smith Development Labs or its work.

A link to another site does not mean we control its content or operation; please review the information presented by that service when you use it.
