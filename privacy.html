The details / your information

Privacy Policy

A plain-language look at the information involved when you reach out, chat, or book time with us.

Effective November 26, 2026
The short version

When you contact Smith Development Labs, LLC, we use the details you share to understand your request and get back to you. Some website and communication tools are provided by other companies, and they may handle information needed to make those tools work. We use cookies and similar technologies on the site, and we do not sell your personal information.

The sections below explain the types of information involved and how you can contact us with questions.

01 / Who we are

Smith Development Labs, LLC is based in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania. This policy concerns information involved in visiting our website or contacting us about our work. Questions about privacy can be sent to msmith@smithdevlabs.com.

02 / Information involved

Depending on how you reach us, information may include:

  • Details you provide in an inquiry, such as your name, business, email address, phone number, website, and project details.
  • Messages and conversation content you share through website chat or an AI assistant, by email, or during a call inquiry.
  • Information you provide when booking a conversation, such as your contact details, selected time, and any notes.
  • Technical usage information that the services providing the site or its tools may collect as part of operating them.

Please avoid including sensitive information in an initial inquiry or chat unless it is necessary to explain your request.

03 / Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website and its contact tools, to remember your preferences (such as dismissing a cookie banner), and to understand how the site is used. Some of these technologies are set by third-party services described in the Tools and service providers section.

You can decline analytics and other non-essential cookies through the banner presented on the site, and you can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Our chat and booking tools load when you visit because they are needed for those features to work, and they may set their own cookies or similar technologies. Declining non-essential cookies does not turn these tools off. If you prefer not to use them, you can email us at msmith@smithdevlabs.com instead.

04 / How information is used

We use inquiry and conversation details to respond, discuss your needs, schedule conversations, provide information you request, and operate and improve the website and its contact tools. Information you share in a chat, including an AI-assisted chat, may be used to follow up on that conversation.

05 / Calls and the AI receptionist

When you call and our AI receptionist answers, the call is recorded and transcribed. We use the recording, transcript and summary to understand your request, follow up with you, and keep business records. They are stored in our business systems, where we review them and add notes. For calls we take live, we only record if we tell you first and you stay on the call. If you'd prefer not to be recorded, you can email us at msmith@smithdevlabs.com instead.

06 / How we share information; no sale

We share information only with the service providers described in this policy, as needed to operate the website and respond to you, or where required by law. We do not sell personal information. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

07 / How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and conversation details only as long as needed to respond to your request and for legitimate business records, after which we delete or anonymize them, unless a longer period is required by law.

08 / Tools and service providers

Services involved in operating the website and responding to inquiries may handle information for those purposes. These can include HighLevel/LeadConnector for forms, chat, and booking; GitHub Pages and/or Cloudflare Pages for website hosting; and Google Workspace for email. The tools available on the site may change, and some may not be available on every version of the site.

Externally hosted tools may use their own technologies and are subject to their own privacy practices. Review any notice presented within a tool when using it.

09 / Inquiry messages and optional marketing

If you contact us or request an appointment, we may reply about your inquiry or booking using the contact details you provide, including appointment confirmations or reminders sent by SMS or email. These are transactional messages and are separate from optional promotional messages.

Promotional email or SMS would be sent only if you explicitly opt in through the relevant channel. Agreeing to promotional messages is not a condition of sending an inquiry or booking a conversation. If you have opted in, you can opt out of promotional SMS by replying STOP; for promotional email, use an unsubscribe option where one is provided or email msmith@smithdevlabs.com.

10 / Your privacy requests

You can email msmith@smithdevlabs.com to ask about information associated with your inquiry or to request access, correction, or deletion. We will review requests in light of applicable law and legitimate business needs. Please provide enough detail to help us locate the relevant information.

11 / Children and questions

This website is not directed to children. If you have questions about this policy or the information described here, contact msmith@smithdevlabs.com.
